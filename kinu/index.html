01 — Philosophy

Less,
but considered.

KINU follows the Japanese idea of shun—enjoying each ingredient at the brief moment it is at its best.

Our menu moves with the Belgian seasons and is shaped by Japanese technique. We source closely, waste little and let each element speak without embellishment.
