01 Our place

Small room.
Big welcome.

A café made for the pause between one thing and the next.

Since
2024

FOS means “light” in Greek. Here, it means a bright corner, a properly pulled espresso, and nowhere you need to rush to.

We work with independent Belgian roasters, nearby bakers, and ingredients that follow the season. Nothing too fussy. Everything made with attention.

Bring a friend, a book, a half-finished idea—or just yourself.
